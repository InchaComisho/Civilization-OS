# Civilization OS Repository Guide

[日本語版はこちら / Japanese version](REPOSITORY_GUIDE_ja.md)

## Purpose of This Repository

This repository defines **Civilization OS** as the deep operating layer of human civilization.

It does not treat climate change, resource depletion, soil degradation, water scarcity, social fragmentation, urban fragility, or AI risk as isolated problems.  
Instead, it interprets them as symptoms of a deeper structural issue: the operating principles that determine how civilization uses knowledge, technology, institutions, energy, cities, food systems, water systems, and artificial intelligence.

The core question of this repository is:

> What operating system is civilization running on?

---

## Role within the Civilization OS Network

This repository is the **core definition layer** of the Civilization OS group.

It connects three levels:

1. **Diagnosis**  
   The current civilization model is driven by linear thinking, consumption-based development, anthropocentrism, externalization of nature, growth-first economics, and short-term optimization.

2. **Definition**  
   Civilization OS is the deep layer of assumptions, values, institutions, technologies, infrastructures, and worldviews that determines how civilization operates.

3. **Transition**  
   The next-stage Civilization OS must be connected to natural law, planetary circulation, biosphere stability, urban redesign, distributed infrastructure, and AI value alignment.

## Conceptual Structure

```text
Worldview
  ↓
Values
  ↓
Institutions
  ↓
Science and Technology
  ↓
Infrastructure
  ↓
Economy and Resource Flows
  ↓
Cities, Food, Water, Energy, AI
  ↓
Civilization Outcome
```

Civilization OS is the layer that determines the direction of all downstream systems.

If the operating principles are extractive, linear, and short-term, even advanced technology may accelerate collapse.  
If the operating principles are circular, restorative, and aligned with natural law, technology can become a tool for civilization recovery.
